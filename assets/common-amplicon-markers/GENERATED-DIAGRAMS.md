# Generated marker diagrams

These PNGs were created with the built-in image-generation tool on 8 November 2026 for educational use. The original JPEGs remain alongside them. Diagrams are conceptual, not to scale or sequence-aligned. Primer names denote examples; binding positions, coverage, amplicon lengths and taxonomy vary with primer variant and organism. Verify against reference sequences and the intended protocol before primer selection or experimental use. The fungal ITS1F–ITS4 product includes 5.8S between ITS1 and ITS2; the simplified tracks do not show every base of flanking rDNA.

## Generation prompts

All images used the built-in image-generation tool with a scientific-educational, landscape, white-background, clean vector-like style, large sans-serif labels, navy/teal/amber palette, inward-facing primer arrows, and the footer “Schematic; not to scale.” Individual prompts specified:

- **16S:** V1–V9 in order; three tracks for 27F/1492R, 341F/805R and 515F/806R.
- **16S–23S:** 16S rRNA, 16S–23S spacer and 23S rRNA in order; tracks for 27F/1492R and 27F/23SR.
- **18S:** V1–V9 in order; TAReuk454FWD1/TAReukREV3 at V4 and 1389F/1510R at V9.
- **Fungal ribosomal DNA:** 18S, ITS1, 5.8S, ITS2 and 28S in order; ITS1/ITS4 and ITS3/ITS4.
- **Fungal ITS:** same five regions; ITS1F/ITS2, ITS3/ITS4 and ITS1F/ITS4.
- **COI:** COI and Folmer barcode region; LCO1490/HCO2198 and mlCOIintF/jgHCO2198.
- **Fish 12S:** 12S rRNA with MiFish target and MiFish-U-F/MiFish-U-R.
- **Plant trnL:** 5′ exon, intron containing the P6 loop, 3′ exon; c/d spanning the intron and g/h spanning P6.
